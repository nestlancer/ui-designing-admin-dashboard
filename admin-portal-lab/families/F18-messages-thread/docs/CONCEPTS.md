# F18 · Five redesign concepts (review pass, 2 Nov 2026)

All variants retain the same two source-separated fixtures: screenshot-backed Payments ops group and HTML-backed Amit Verma direct thread. The group has the three screenshot-visible participants; the direct record has no borrowed group details. Actions remain local previews; no invented IDs or mutations. Teal tokens stay locked.

| Variant | Concept | Distinct composition | Job supported |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Three-pane desk** | Queue, transcript and context share the canvas. | Keep worklist, messages and group/direct context in view. |
| V2 | **Thread reader** | Transcript receives most width; a slim queue rail preserves orientation; context is toggled. | Read/reply preview against the message chronology. |
| V3 | **Room directory** | Fixture switch and group participants lead above the transcript; context emphasizes source identity. | Inspect who/which source this record belongs to before reading. |
| V4 | **Message ledger** | Compact chronology and actor/time register with a secondary details rail. | Scan captured messages and events in dense operations contexts. |
| V5 | **Conversation folio** | Dated narrative spine and generous message width with subordinate context. | Read longer thread content with clear date/source landmarks. |

## Difference-test intent

The variants differ in pane geometry, reading order, density and context placement—not palette. Every design must preserve message ordering, original group/direct separation, mobile list-first behavior, reduced-motion support and no-request/no-permission/no-audit disclosure. Browser and keyboard tests remain outstanding; these concept notes do not mean Gate 3 has passed.

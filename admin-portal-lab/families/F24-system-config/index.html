What is deliberately not invented

The capture reports 62 templates: 10 email + 52 notification. Only 11 identities and one email body are recoverable. The 51 missing notification identities and 9 missing email bodies remain explicit source gaps, not fabricated records.

Safe prototypes, not production controls

All edits, permissions, test requests, cache actions and receipts are local demonstrations. No email is sent, no cache is cleared, and no server ACL or audit trail is claimed. Keep the relative-asset bundle together and open designs through the live preview.
